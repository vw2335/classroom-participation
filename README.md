# Classroom Participation

## How to use

1. Open the [live prototype](https://before-someone-speaks.valiyw207.chatgpt.site), or download this repository and open `index.html` in a browser. No installation is needed.
2. Hover over a student circle to reveal their private thought. On a touchscreen, tap once to reveal it.
3. Click any student to make them the first speaker. On a touchscreen, tap that student again.
4. Watch participation spread gradually through speech bubbles and brighter circles. Two students remain quietly thinking.
5. Select **Reset** to return to the initial silence and try a different first speaker.

## Phenomenon

Classes can appear completely silent even when several students already have thoughts or questions. The first person who speaks provides a social cue that can lower uncertainty and make participation feel safer for others.

## Context

A seminar/discussion-based class after the instructor asks open-ended questions. Several students may already have something to contribute while the others are watching the room and deciding whether to speak.

## Pattern

silence → hesitation → one person speaks → inner thoughts → everyone speaks

## Relationships

Engagement from crowd and audience might affect other students.

## Main representation

A group of simple student circles. At first, everyone appears silent. Hovering over each student reveals a private thought such as “I have an idea” or “I’m not sure if this is right.” When the user clicks one student to speak, other students gradually become more active through speech bubbles, brighter circles, or connecting lines.

## What it preserves

The changing of attitude from different “side” of the classroom and the gap between private thinking and public participation, and how one person’s action can affect the whole group.

## What it leaves out

Whether answers are correct, students’ academic ability, and all the different personal reasons someone may choose not to speak.

## Final product

A simple HTML/CSS/JavaScript interactive classroom where users can reveal students’ hidden thoughts and trigger the first speaker to see how the participation pattern changes.

## Educational meaning

Classroom participation is not only about individual confidence. It is also shaped by the social environment and the behavior of other students.
